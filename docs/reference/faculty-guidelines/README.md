# Faculty PBL Guidelines & Reference Documents

This directory contains the original, authoritative Problem-Based Learning (PBL) assessment guidelines and evaluation rubric documents provided by the faculty for **24UADPC302: Predictive Modeling Using SPSS (IBM)**, Department of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (TY AIDS), Sanjivani University, Kopargaon.

## Reference Documents

1. **`Assignment_4144_Content_Document_20261019020930913PM.pdf`**
   - Official PBL project instructions, problem framing, and assessment expectations.
2. **`Assignment_4144_Content_SolutionDocument_20261019020930913PM (1).pdf`**
   - Detailed 100-mark evaluation rubric spanning all nine required criteria.

## Faculty Evaluation Rubric (100 Marks Total)

1. **Problem Definition & Business Understanding** — 10 Marks
2. **Dataset & Data Understanding** — 10 Marks
3. **Data Preprocessing & Cleaning** — 10 Marks
4. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** — 10 Marks
5. **Feature Engineering / Selection** — 10 Marks
6. **Model Selection & Implementation** — 15 Marks
7. **Model Evaluation & Validation** — 15 Marks
8. **Business Insights & Recommendations** — 10 Marks
9. **Documentation & Presentation** — 10 Marks

*Note:* These documents serve as the governing academic evaluation standard throughout the project lifecycle. They must be consulted during all milestone audits and reviews.
